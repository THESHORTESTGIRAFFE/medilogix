# MediLogix Release Notes - v1.1.0

**Release Date:** 2025-10-22

## ✨ New Features

### 🛡️ Blockchain-Enhanced Audit Trails

To significantly boost security and data integrity, we have introduced a new **Blockchain-Based Audit Trail** feature. This creates an immutable, verifiable log for all critical inventory and procurement records, including:

- Stock Movements
- Purchase Orders
- Goods Received Notes (GRNs)

Each transaction is now cryptographically linked to the previous one, making the entire chain tamper-resistant. This ensures that once a record is created, it cannot be altered without breaking the chain, providing an unprecedented level of trust and accountability for all stakeholders, including the Ministry of Health and Child Care (MoHCC) and donor organizations.

A new internal tool has also been developed for administrators to verify the integrity of these chains at any time, ensuring continuous compliance and security.

## 🐞 Bug Fixes

- **System Stability:** Resolved several critical `NameError` bugs that were preventing certain application modules, such as invoice management, from loading correctly. These fixes have improved the overall stability and reliability of the platform.
- **Database Connectivity:** Addressed and resolved initial database connection issues to ensure smooth and reliable communication with the application's backend.

## 🔜 What's Next?

We are now shifting our focus to **Phase 2: Advanced Inventory Management**, which will introduce features like:

- **Barcode & QR Code Integration** for faster, error-free data entry.
- **Enhanced Batch & Expiry Tracking** to minimize waste.
- A comprehensive **Stock Transfer Module** for inter-facility logistics.

These upcoming features will further streamline warehouse operations and bring even greater efficiency to the national medical supply chain.
